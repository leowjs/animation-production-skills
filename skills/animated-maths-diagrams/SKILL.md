# Animated Maths Diagrams

Read the [repository catalog](../video-production/references/repositories.md), the matching [example prompt](../video-production/references/example-prompts.md), and the relevant [production checks](../video-production/references/production-checks.md).

**Opus video prompt packs is the first creative reference for maths and line-art diagrams.** Use examples to study sequencing and annotation. Verify the reasoning independently and write original scene instructions. Select SVG, Canvas, or an existing project renderer based on the output.

1. Write the mathematical or scientific claim in plain language. Check assumptions, arithmetic, notation, and where an example stops short of proving a general claim.
2. Assign one change to each beat. Preserve the relation between the diagram, labels, and equations. If the duration would force a key step to disappear too quickly, simplify the scene or choose a longer cut.
3. Inspect a frame at each reasoning step and at the final conclusion. For an exported video, verify captions and numerical labels in the actual render.
